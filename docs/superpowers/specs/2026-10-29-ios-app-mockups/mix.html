A · Engraved dial

Closest to Banknote. The dial face carries the guilloché engraving, the needle is ink-thin, and the score sits in a serif at the dial's hub like a mint mark. Rows stay ruled, the suggestion stays in a green panel.

  • Most distinctive; nobody else's finance app looks like this
  • Serif numbers everywhere — formal, a little old-world
  • The engraving needs care at small sizes (the widget drops it)

B · Instrument on paper

Closest to Gauge, recolored. A clean ticked dial with an amber needle, the narrow meter numerals from Gauge, and the five parts as a row of readouts — all printed in Banknote's greens on sage paper.

  • Fastest to read at a glance; the dial is the hero
  • Meter numerals feel technical and precise
  • Less ornament, so it relies on color and spacing for character

C · Vault dial

The dial lives in a note-green header, like the face of a safe: the arc fills in amber up to your score, ticks are etched in pale green, and the number is set large in the serif. Below the fold it's calm sage paper with ruled rows.

  • Strongest first impression; reads as premium
  • Fill-up arc instead of a needle — simpler, still a scale
  • The dark header makes the widget a matching green tile
